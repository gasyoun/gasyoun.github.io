Study tool 05 · how a Sanskrit word is built

Word formation

Wiktionary’s etymology tags say that a word is a compound, a derivative or a vṛddhi formation; they rarely show the machinery. This page brings in three earlier datasets — an inventory of pratyayas with their anubandha rules, a four-class samāsa taxonomy, and statistics on which stems actually build compounds — and keeps each one tied to its own source and to the Wiktionary categories it illustrates.
