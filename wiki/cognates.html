What the cells mean. Each cell lists short reflexes found as real page titles in that language's Wiktionary category, plus a link to the full category. A category also contains later derivatives, so membership is evidence of a shared source, not proof that every listed word is a direct cognate. Asterisked forms and Reconstruction: entries are scholarly reconstructions, not attested words.

03 · teaching notes

What to say in the classroom

  • Cognate is not the same as loanword. Sanskrit भृ and English bear continue a common ancestor; Sanskrit terms borrowed from Ancient Greek entered by contact. Wiktionary keeps the two routes in different category families — that distinction is the whole point of the etymology tree.
  • Correspondences are regular, not vaguely similar. In the *bʰer- set, PIE *bʰ appears as Sanskrit bh, Greek ph (φ), Latin f and Germanic b. Ask students to check this against the panel above rather than take it on trust.
  • Reconstruction is a hypothesis with a notation. The asterisk marks a form no text attests. Wiktionary's Reconstruction: namespace holds these separately; the map labels them so students never confuse a model with a manuscript.
  • Absence of a cell is absence of a category. These are editorial buckets on a living wiki. An empty cell says nothing about the historical language — only that nobody has filed that combination yet.
  • Continue to the timeline. The Timeline of layers shows the same material as successive inheritance strata with their real word counts.
